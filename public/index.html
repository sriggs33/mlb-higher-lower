How to play

  1. Two players, one stat. The player on the left shows their number.
  2. Call it: is the player on the right Higher or Lower?
  3. The left player stays on top until a challenger has a higher number, or until they've faced 3–4 challengers. Then the challenger slides over.
  4. The stat switches every 3 guesses.
  5. You have 3 lives. Each wrong call costs one; lose all three and the run is over.
  6. Your score is how many you got right. Matchups get tighter the deeper you go. Nail all 30 for a perfect game.

Four dailies a day: Batters or Pitchers, this season or all-time. Everyone gets the same players. New puzzles at midnight. Endless mode is unlimited practice.
